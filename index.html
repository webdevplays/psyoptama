About HookStoken

HookStoken celebrates the revolutionary power of Uniswap v4's hooks technology that's transforming DeFi. When v4 launched in January 2025, it shattered records by hitting $1 billion TVL in just 21 days—twice as fast as v3.

With over 5,000 hooks deployed by December, developers discovered they could finally inject custom logic into pools, enabling dynamic fees, MEV protection, and automated rebalancing that seemed impossible before.

HookStoken isn't just another meme coin. It's a tribute to the innovation that's turning Uniswap from a rigid DEX into a flexible playground for DeFi builders. We're here to ride the hook wave and bring the community along for the journey.
